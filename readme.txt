Club management for dog breeding clubs: stud dogs with pedigree, litters, dogs to place, approvals, breeding licenses, results, shows and more.

== Description ==

Rudelwerk provides everything a dog breeding club needs on its website:

* Stud dogs with a three-generation pedigree, breeding license and approval
* Litters (born and expected) with parents, photos and availability
* Dogs to place, approvals, breeding licenses, results, shows
* Trimming proofs, dog sitters, literature and vacation deals
* Kennels, breeders and owners with contact details
* Stud dog filter (ZIP code, size, HD, ED, exams, titles, approval)
* Shortcodes for overview pages and seven "recent entries" widgets
* Theme independent output with overridable templates

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `rudelwerk` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate the plugin through the "Plugins" screen.
3. Migrating from the "Extra Child" theme? See `docs/MIGRATION.md`.
